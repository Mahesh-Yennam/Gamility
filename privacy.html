Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At Gamility (accessible at https://gamility.com/), protecting your privacy is our core priority. This document outlines the types of information handled by Gamility and how we safeguard your personal data.

1. Client-Side Processing Guarantee

Our utility tools (including the Word Counter, Password Generator, QR Code Maker, Unit Converter, JSON Formatter, and Color Picker) are designed to run 100% locally inside your web browser using JavaScript. Any text, passwords, strings, or images you paste or generate in these tools remain strictly on your device and are never transmitted to, collected by, or stored on our servers.

2. Information We Collect

Gamility is an open web platform. We do not require account registration, passwords, or profile setup to use our games or tools.

  • Contact Form Data: When you voluntarily submit a message or suggestion via our Contact form, your name, email address, and message are securely delivered to our team via FormSubmit.co for the sole purpose of responding to your inquiry.
  • Anonymous Log Files: Like standard web hosts, our static hosting provider may log anonymous technical data (such as browser type, IP address, referring pages, and timestamp) for server diagnostic and security monitoring purposes.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

Gamility does not use tracking or behavioral advertising cookies. Certain browser games may utilize HTML5 localStorage purely to remember your local game high score or volume preferences on your own device.

4. Third-Party Links

Gamility may contain links to external websites or third-party resources. We have no control over the content, privacy practices, or policies of third-party websites and encourage you to review their respective privacy statements.

5. Children's Privacy Protection

Gamility does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Our games and tools are designed for general public entertainment and utility.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new site features. We advise you to review this page occasionally for updates.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact us via our Contact Page.
